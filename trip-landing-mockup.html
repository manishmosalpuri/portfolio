About This Trip

Lake Fork is one of the most productive bass fisheries in North America — home to over 50 state records and consistently producing fish over 10 lbs. On this 6-hour guided trip, you'll fish with an experienced local guide who knows the lake intimately, targeting largemouth bass using proven techniques adapted to current conditions.

Whether you're a first-time angler or a tournament veteran, Captain Jake tailors the experience to your skill level. He'll teach you to read the water, work structure, and set the hook properly — or if you know what you're doing, he'll put you on fish and get out of the way.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need a fishing licence? +
Yes — Texas requires a valid fishing licence for all anglers age 17 and older. You can purchase one online at tpwd.texas.gov or at any local bait shop. A one-day licence costs approximately $11. Captain Jake can point you to the nearest retailer if needed.
What if the weather is bad? +
Light rain doesn't stop fishing — bass often bite better when it's overcast. If severe weather (lightning, high winds) makes the trip unsafe, Captain Jake will contact you to reschedule at no charge. Texas weather is predictable 48 hours out and we'll reach out if there's a concern.
What's the cancellation policy? +
Free cancellation up to 48 hours before your trip for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours receive a 50% refund or the option to reschedule. No-shows are not refundable.
What should I bring? +
Sun protection (hat, sunscreen, long sleeves recommended), sunglasses with polarised lenses, snacks and drinks, a valid fishing licence, and a camera. Non-slip shoes are strongly recommended. All fishing gear is provided.
What if we don't catch fish? +
Lake Fork consistently produces fish year-round, but fishing — like all outdoor activities — has unpredictable days. Captain Jake will work every angle to put you on fish. No guide can guarantee catches, but he has a strong track record and 47 reviews speak to the experience.
